This isn't couples therapy for people in trouble. It's a set of conversations
that are much easier to have now than in year three.

Money and what you each assume about it. Family, and how much access they get.
Whether you want children and what happens if one of you changes their mind.
How you each behave when you're stressed, and what you need when the other one
is.

Plenty of couples find they agree on more than they expected. The point isn't
to find problems, it's to stop discovering the important stuff by accident.
