Most people arrive able to explain their problem clearly. They've read about
it, they can name the pattern, and it keeps happening anyway. Understanding
something and being free of it turn out to be different jobs.

We meet weekly to start, because that's what builds momentum, and momentum is
most of the work. Once things feel steadier we can space it out.

You don't need a diagnosis to come, and you don't need to have hit a crisis.
"This isn't terrible but I don't want to keep living like this" is a completely
good reason.
