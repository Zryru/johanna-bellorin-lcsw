Most couples don't come in with a hundred problems. They come in with one or
two, on a loop, wearing different outfits each time.

My job isn't to decide who's right. It's to slow the loop down enough that you
can both see what's actually happening in it, and to make the room safe enough
that the honest version of the conversation becomes possible.

You can come as a couple whether or not you're married, whether or not you live
together, and whether or not you're sure you want to stay together. That last
one is a legitimate thing to work on rather than something to resolve before
you arrive.
