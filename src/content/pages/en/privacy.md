## Website privacy policy

This section is about you as a visitor to this website, not as a client.

**What this site collects.** If you send the contact form, it collects your
name, email, phone number if you provide it, your preferred language, and the
reason you selected. That information is used only to reply to you. It is not
sold, and it is not shared with advertisers.

**Analytics.** This site uses privacy-first analytics that do not use cookies
and do not identify you personally. No advertising or tracking pixels are used
anywhere on this site.

**The contact form is for logistics, not clinical detail.** Please do not
include confidential health information in it. For anything clinical, we use a
secure channel, not email or web forms.

## Accessibility

This site aims to meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Color contrast has been checked across the
site. If you run into a barrier using it, please reach out through the contact
page and it will be addressed.

Full conformance with accessibility standards requires testing with assistive
technology and expert review, which is ongoing.
