## How a session actually goes

I use humor when it fits. I make pop culture references. I dress casually and
I talk to you like a person, because that's what I am and that's what you are.
You can swear. You can eat something. You can say "I don't want to talk about
that today" and we'll go somewhere else.

None of that makes the work lighter. We're going to talk about serious things
and some of it is going to be hard. It just means you won't be doing that in a
cold room with someone who sounds like a textbook.

## What matters most to me

The relationship. Not as a nice extra, as the actual thing that makes therapy
work. For me to be any use to you, this has to feel like a safe place.

It's not a friendship. In a friendship the support goes both ways; here it's
about you, all of it. But I want enough trust between us that you can tell me
when something isn't working. "Hey Johanna, I don't want to do that." "I
didn't like what you just said." Those sentences are welcome. They're
information, not a problem.
